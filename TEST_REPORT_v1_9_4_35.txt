Behavior implemented
- GPS watch starts independently of destination/route/follow mode.
- Guidance stop does not stop GPS watch.
- If watch fixes are stale >6.5s while foregrounded, high-accuracy getCurrentPosition is used as a recovery poll.
- Foreground/pageshow recovery requests a fresh fix when stale.
- Mobile home displays real-time km/h without a route.
- coords.speed fallback uses point-to-point distance/time with accuracy-based jitter suppression.
- Current live speed and learned ETA walking speed are separated.
- No-route shadow render refresh: >=8m movement / limited to >=1.4s cadence, plus 5s stale threshold.
- Current GPS environment prefetch: 360m building/vegetation, >=45m movement or ~10s stale, >=4.5s request cadence.
- Viewport building debounce: 90ms (previously 140ms).

Requires real-device validation
- Android Chrome / Samsung Internet GPS event cadence varies by OS power/location settings.
- Background tabs may be throttled by the browser/OS; foreground recovery is implemented.
- Live speed quality depends on GPS accuracy and device-provided coords.speed.
- Faster shadow/environment refresh should be checked for battery/network impact during a 10–20 minute walk.
